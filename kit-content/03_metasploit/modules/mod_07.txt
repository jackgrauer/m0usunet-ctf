TARGETS   Jenkins 2.137 and earlier; LTS 2.121.1 and earlier

DESCRIPTION

  exposure of method calls on Jenkins core security objects. A
  specially crafted URL to descriptorByName allows execution of
  arbitrary Groovy in the SecureGroovyScript sandbox, which can be
  trivially escaped.
